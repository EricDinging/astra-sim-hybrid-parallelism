1 · The real machine in one picture

Cubes are interchangeable atoms. Fixed electrical wiring inside each N×N×N cube; every link between cubes is an optical circuit. Distance does not exist (R4) — the only scarce thing is that each surface port holds one circuit at a time (R5). The "big torus" is just one switch configuration.

2 · The rules (R1–R7)

rulestatement
R1 cubes are atomsN×N×N chips, static electrical mesh inside; never rewired.
R2 optical seams onlyOnly surface chips have an outward port, and every such port goes to an OCS. Interior chips can never leave the cube.
R3 legality (corrected)Same axis + same within-face position (i,j). Cross-cube: any polarity (+x↔−x and +x↔+x). Same cube: only +x(i,j)↔−x(i,j) — the one-cube-wide ring wrap.
R4 no proximityAny two distinct cubes are potentially one OCS hop apart; floor position is meaningless. Potential, not standing, connectivity.
R5 port exclusivityOne circuit per port at a time — the entire resource problem.
R6 free ring closureThe wrap link of a chain is an ordinary circuit.
R7 cube-granular, isolated slicesReal jobs get whole-cube private tori; no shared fabric between slices.

3 · Simulator dispositions (S1–S3)

assumptiondispositionmeaning
S1 static base torus with free adjacent seams REVISED Default torus = initial OCS configuration, not hardware. Fully implemented: no ranking key prices circuits or default seams, and realizability follows corrected R3 (gated on ocs_enabled; false only at whole-torus block, where no OCS exists).
S2 sub-cube placements, arbitrary even shapes KEPT (extension) Research question: what would a TPU-style cluster gain from sub-cube slices, and what does support cost?
S3 shared fabric (multi-hop default routing) KEPT (extension) Same framing: deliberately beyond R7 isolation to study what shared-fabric support takes.

4 · What the experiments settled

Fragmentation, not contiguity, is the thing to protect. The 2026-07-19 experiment that made "non-contiguous competes as an equal" look catastrophic (mean JCT 7.2 s → 43–65 s) predated tile packing: origin-locked tiles sprayed fragments into idle cubes. With cube sharing and the fragmented-cube-first order in place, dropping the contiguity tier won every probe cell (5k jobs: fifo-0.60 −4.8%, fifo-0.90 −15%, easy-0.90 −2.5%). The shipped ranking is tier-free: a placement pays only for cubes it takes from fully idle to partially occupied.

Remaining conservatism: polarity-free pairings are legal in the model, but no search yet exploits mirrored tile traversal.

5 · Implementation status (2026-07-22)

Final state (2026-07-22, 290/290 tests): a single rfold (rfoldv1 and all superseded study arms deleted). Corrected-R3 predicates · R5 port-exclusivity fix (the new legality checker caught real placements putting two circuits on one port) · best-effort wiring for non-contiguous placement (unwirable edges ride the shared fabric; before this, only side lengths {1,2,4,8} could place non-contiguously — the whole 72-chip family could not) · tile packing with cube sharing (per-tile offsets, fragmented-cube-first) · fragmentation-first ranking (frag, cubes, multihop, cost) — the contiguity tier lost every probe cell (up to −15% mean JCT) and is gone · micro-benchmark layer (rdcn_check.hh + microbench_rfold).
